20 WILD WEAPONS

Rockets, cluster bombs, bomb arrows, buzzsaws, a wrecking ball on a chain, quake hammers, anti-grav fields, black holes, a nuke… Drag one onto the tower, aim, let go.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Real physics

Every block is real: knock out the right support and watch the whole tower come crashing down.

Castles & shields

Storm fortresses with corner towers and a keep. Some hide under a shield dome: take out the reactors first.

Daily challenge

The same towers and the same weapons in the same order for everyone. Plan your shots and share your result.

Endless

Fly from island to island as far as you can. Every island is a checkpoint.

Share the boom

Your best collapse becomes a short video, made on your phone, ready to share.

Five languages

THE VILLAIN

“A flying rowboat? How adorable.”

Baron Bolt has closed the sky route with a gun tower on every island. You have a flying galley and 20 ways to reopen it: seven island worlds from Meadow Isle to Candy Cove, ending at his last hideout.
