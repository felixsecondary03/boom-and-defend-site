What the game stores

Your progress (stars, coins, unlocked weapons, trophies, best scores and settings) is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your phone and is not sent to us or anyone else. Your phone's own backup (Android backup with your Google account, or iCloud / computer backup on iPhone) may include it, so it can come back on a new phone; that is handled by Google or Apple under your account settings.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or personal information.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No network requests: the game runs fully offline.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • No in-app purchases.

Google Play and the App Store

When you download the game, Google (Play Store, policies.google.com/privacy) or Apple (App Store, apple.com/legal/privacy) processes data as the operator of the store under its own privacy policy. We receive only anonymous, aggregated statistics from them (for example install counts and, if you agreed to share them, crash reports).

This web page

When you open this page, the hosting provider processes technical access data (such as your IP address and the time of access) to deliver the page and keep it secure. We do not use cookies or analytics on this page.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since the game processes no personal data, we usually hold nothing about you; you can contact us at any time with questions.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app (on iPhone: delete the app).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with an app update and on this page.

Was das Spiel speichert

Dein Spielstand (Sterne, Münzen, freigeschaltete Waffen, Trophäen, Bestwerte und Einstellungen) wird nur auf deinem Gerät im lokalen Speicher der App gespeichert. Er verlässt dein Handy nie und wird weder an uns noch an Dritte übertragen. Die Sicherung deines Handys (Android-Backup über dein Google-Konto oder iCloud-/Computer-Backup beim iPhone) kann ihn enthalten, damit er auf einem neuen Gerät zurückkommt; dafür sind Google bzw. Apple nach den Einstellungen deines Kontos verantwortlich.

Was das Spiel nicht tut

  • Keine Konten, keine Anmeldung, keine persönlichen Angaben.
  • Keine Werbung, keine Analyse, kein Tracking, keine Drittanbieter-SDKs, die Daten erheben.
  • Keine Netzwerkzugriffe: Das Spiel läuft vollständig offline.
  • Kein Zugriff auf Standort, Kontakte, Kamera, Mikrofon oder Dateien.
  • Keine In-App-Käufe.
